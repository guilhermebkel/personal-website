# Copy standards

How to write what a visitor reads: pages, buttons, links, `aria-label`, page titles, the 404 and the metadata. The vocabulary is in `GLOSSARY.md`; the posts follow their own voice (`docs/content.md`) and are the owner's.

## Voice

- **First person singular, in the owner's voice.** "Notes on what I build, break and rebuild." The site is Guilherme's, so it says "I", never "we" and never "the author".
- **Plain and direct.** One idea per sentence, sentence case, no filler, no hype. Say what the thing is.
- **A little dry.** The 404 says "Nothing here. I probably moved it." A joke is allowed where nothing is at stake; an error or a form never jokes.
- **No claims.** The site exposes work; it does not sell it. No "world-class", "passionate" or "innovative".

## Words

| Use                                             | Do not use                        |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| projects, posts                                 | portfolio, case studies, articles |
| Read the posts                                  | Click here, See more here         |
| About                                           | Who am I                          |
| contact links by name (GitHub, LinkedIn, email) | "get in touch" as a button label  |

A term that has a meaning in `GLOSSARY.md` (post, project, content) is used only with that meaning.

## Buttons and links

- A link or a button says what happens: a verb and its object ("Read the posts"), or the name of the place ("Posts", "About").
- The same action keeps the same name everywhere.
- A link that leaves the site names the destination, not "link".

## Titles and metadata

- The page title is `<page> · Guilherme Mota`; the home is `Guilherme Mota`.
- A description is one sentence, under 160 characters, and says what is on the page.
- `<html lang="en">` is set once in the layout; text in another language (a quote) carries its own `lang`.

## Errors and empty states

- A 404 or an empty list says what is missing and where to go next, in one or two short sentences.
- No apology and no raw code.

## Before finishing

Read the text aloud. If a sentence needs a second read, shorten it.
